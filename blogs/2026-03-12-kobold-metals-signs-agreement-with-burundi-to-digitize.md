KoBold Metals has signed an agreement with the Government of Burundi to support the digitization of the country’s geological data. The agreement was signed in Washington, D.C., during a ceremony hosted by the U.S. Department of State and presided over by Deputy Assistant Secretary of State for African Affairs Sarah Troutman.
