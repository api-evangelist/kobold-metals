# KoBold Metals

> KoBold Metals is a critical-minerals exploration and development company that applies artificial intelligence and machine learning to the search for copper, lithium, nickel and cobalt deposits. It combines novel sensor hardware/software, centralized geological data systems, and predictive targeting models to "turn exploration into a repeatable science." Its flagship project is the Mingomba copper deposit in Zambia. KoBold is a private, venture-backed company (investors include a16z and Bond Capital).

KoBold Metals publishes **no public developer API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable specification** as of 2026-07-19. There is no `/.well-known/` discovery surface and no `/llms.txt` on the corporate host.
